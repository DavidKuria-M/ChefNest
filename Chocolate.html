Chocolate Cake

This homemade chocolate cake recipe is super easy to prepare in one bowl for a deliciously moist and fluffy chocolate cake.

Ingredients:

  1. 2 cups white sugar: White sugar helps keep the cake moist and tender.
  2. 1 ¾ cups all-purpose flour: All-purpose flour provides structure and gives the cake its shape.
  3. ¾ cup unsweetened cocoa powder: Unsweetened cocoa powder adds rich chocolate flavor and color.
  4. 1 ½ teaspoons baking powder: This helps the cake rise and become light and fluffy.
  5. 1 ½ teaspoons baking soda: Added to improve rise and texture.
  6. 1 teaspoon salt: A little salt enhances the chocolate flavor and balances the sweetness.
  7. 2 large eggs: Help bind the batter together.
  8. 1 cup milk: Adds moisture and creates a smooth, pourable batter.
  9. 1/2 cup vegetable oil: Vegetable oil keeps the cake soft and moist longer than butter would.
  10. 2 teaspoons vanilla extract: Enhances and rounds out the chocolate flavor.
  11. 1 cup boiling water: Creates a thinner batter for a moist, tender crumb.

Instructions:

  • Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Grease and flour two 9-inch round baking pans.
  • Stir sugar, flour, cocoa, baking powder, baking soda, and salt together in a large bowl.
  • Add eggs, milk, oil and vanilla; mix for 2 minutes on medium speed with an electric mixer.
  • Stir in the boiling water. The batter will be thin
  • Pour evenly into the prepared pans.
  • Bake in the preheated oven until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean, about 30 to 35 minutes. Cool in the pans for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
  • Frost with your favorite chocolate frosting and enjoy!
